Entry for the make.fun bounty [The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23) — one sentence, 113 characters, pitching the worst and most useless app imaginable.

## The pitch

> BlowBack relights your birthday candles the moment you blow them out, $1.99 per wish, so no wish ever has to end.

## Why this pitch

- **Specific app, specific useless function:** BlowBack relights birthday candles.
- **Absurd logic:** the ritual act — blowing out candles to make a wish — is undone the instant it happens, for $1.99 per wish.
- **Sounds like a real pitch:** a product name, a clear statement of what the app does, and an audience (anyone with a birthday).
- **One clean sentence:** 113 characters, no padding, no second idea crowding it.
- **Vivid picture:** candles flaring back to life the moment you blow them out, so no wish ever has to end.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else.
